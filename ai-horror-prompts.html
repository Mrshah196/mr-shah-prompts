AI Horror Prompts

Horror prompts run on atmosphere: darkness, fog, decay and one thing that should not be there. Keep the monster half-hidden and let shadows do the work, because suggestion scares more than gore. These prompts work with ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Midjourney and Flux, and suit thumbnails, posters and stories. Dial the fear up or down by changing the light.

How to write better horror prompts

  • Hide the monster: show shadows, eyes or a silhouette.
  • Use fog, darkness and cold colour tones.
  • Add one wrong detail, like eyes where they should not be.
