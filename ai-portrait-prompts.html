AI Portrait Prompts

Portrait prompts are the most popular way to test an AI image model. Describe the subject, the light and the lens, and the model returns a face with real skin texture and depth. Copy any prompt below, change the subject or the setting, and run it in Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana or any image tool.

How to write better portrait prompts

  • Name the lens and distance, such as an 85mm close-up or a 35mm medium shot.
  • Describe the light: golden-hour backlight, soft window light or studio rim light.
  • Ask for natural skin texture and pores to avoid a plastic look.
