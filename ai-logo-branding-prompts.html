AI Logo and Branding Prompts

A good logo prompt is short and specific. Name the brand idea, the shape language and the colours, and ask for a clean flat result on a plain background. These prompts help you test marks, badges and brand sheets before you send anything to a designer.

How to write better logo & branding prompts

  • Ask for a flat vector style on a white background.
  • Limit the palette to two or three colours.
  • Describe the mood of the brand: playful, premium or bold.
