AI 3D CGI Prompts

3D and CGI prompts need render language to look right: name the renderer feel, the materials and the studio lighting. Mention subsurface scattering, reflections and depth of field and the output reads as a real render. These prompts work with ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Midjourney and Flux, and suit characters, products and scenes.

How to write better 3d cgi prompts

  • Name the render style: Pixar-like, octane render or clay.
  • Describe materials: glossy plastic, brushed metal or soft fabric.
  • Add studio lighting and depth of field for realism.
