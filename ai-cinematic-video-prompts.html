AI Cinematic Video Prompts

Video prompts work best when they read like a shot list. State the camera move, the subject action and the mood in one clear scene. The prompts below are written for text-to-video tools such as Veo, Sora and Runway, and you can copy and adapt them in a few seconds.

How to write better cinematic video prompts

  • Pick one camera move per shot: dolly in, slow pan or handheld.
  • Keep a single clear action so the clip stays steady.
  • Mention light, weather and sound mood to set the tone.
