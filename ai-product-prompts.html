AI Product Prompts

Product prompts sell the object before a single word is read. Describe the product, the surface, the lighting and the background, and the shot looks studio-grade. Keep the styling minimal so the product stays the hero of every frame. Use these prompts with ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Midjourney or Flux for e-commerce listings, advertisements, brand catalogues and launch creatives.

How to write better product prompts

  • Keep the background clean so the product pops.
  • Name the surface: marble, silk, water, concrete.
  • Add a lighting mood: softbox, rim light or sunlight.
