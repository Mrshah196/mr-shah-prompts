How to Use Multi-Page Post Publisher

Publish posts to your Facebook Pages from your browser — no app to install.

Multi-Page Post Publisher lets you publish text, photo and video posts to the Facebook Pages you manage, straight from your browser at mrshahai.com/publisher. Here is how it works.

1. Open the tool and log in

Open mrshahai.com/publisher and click "Login with Facebook". Your Pages load automatically — there is nothing to paste.

2. Choose the Pages

In the Pages list, turn ON the Pages you want to post to. Click "Sab select karein" (Select all) to select every Page at once, or turn individual Pages off.

3. Write your post

In the Composer, type your post text. You can also attach a photo or video with the upload box.

4. Publish Now

Click "Publish Now". The tool publishes to every Page you selected and shows the result for each Page — published, or the error if something went wrong.

Good to know. Your login stays only in your browser — tokens are never sent to or stored on any server. While the app is in development mode, posts are visible only to app admins; public visibility needs Meta App Review approval. You can press Disconnect at any time to remove the saved data from your browser.
