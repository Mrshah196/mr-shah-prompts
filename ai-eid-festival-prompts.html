AI Eid & Festival Prompts

Eid and festival prompts capture celebration: glowing lanterns, henna hands, festive tables and moonlit mosques. Name the festival, the key symbols and the mood, and the image feels instantly festive. These prompts work with ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Midjourney and Flux for greeting cards, posts and banners. Swap the symbols to adapt any prompt for Diwali, Christmas or weddings.

How to write better eid & festival prompts

  • Name the festival and its key symbols: lanterns, henna, sweets.
  • Use warm golden light for a festive glow.
  • Leave space at the top for greeting text.
