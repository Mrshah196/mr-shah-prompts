AI Social Media Prompts

Social media prompts help you make posts, covers and ad visuals that fit a feed. Say the platform, the size and the message, and keep the design simple so the text stays readable. Use the prompts below for posts, banners and story graphics, then adjust the colours to match your page.

How to write better social media prompts

  • State the format: square post, vertical story or wide cover.
  • Leave clear space for text and a headline.
  • Match the colours to your brand or page theme.
