AI Photography Prompts

Photography prompts copy the way a real photographer plans a shot. Choose the genre, the lens, the light and the framing, and the image looks like it came from a camera, not a generator. Copy the prompts below for product, street, travel and macro style pictures.

How to write better photography prompts

  • Name the camera style: 50mm street, macro or wide landscape.
  • Add depth of field, like shallow focus with soft bokeh.
  • Describe real light: overcast, blue hour or hard noon sun.
