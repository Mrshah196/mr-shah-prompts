AI Art Illustration Prompts

Illustration prompts unlock hand-crafted styles that photo prompts cannot reach: watercolour, ink, flat vector, storybook art and more. Name the medium, the line quality and the colour mood up front, and the result stays consistent across a series. These prompts work with ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Midjourney and Flux, and you can tweak characters, palettes and backgrounds in seconds.

How to write better art illustration prompts

  • Name the medium first: watercolour, ink, oil or flat vector.
  • Describe the line work and palette for a consistent series.
  • Keep backgrounds simple so the subject stays the hero.
