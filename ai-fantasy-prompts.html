AI Fantasy Prompts

Fantasy prompts give you room for big worlds: castles, creatures, armour and magic. Set the scale first, then add light, weather and one strong focal subject. Use the examples below as a starting point and swap in your own characters and places.

How to write better fantasy prompts

  • Start with one focal subject, then build the world around it.
  • Use light to show magic: glowing runes, fog or firelight.
  • Mention scale, such as a tiny figure in a huge ruin.
