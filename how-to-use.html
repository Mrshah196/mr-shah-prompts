How to Use Mr Shah Prompts

A quick guide to finding, copying and using prompts from mrshahai.com.

Mr Shah Prompts is a free library of 1,000+ copy-and-paste AI prompts for images, cinematic videos, portraits and more, published from mrshahai.com. Here is how to use it, step by step.

1. Browse the categories

The homepage lists 30 categories — portraits, cinematic video, anime, logos, photography, fantasy and more. Pick a category, or open the Categories page to see them all.

2. Open a prompt card

Each category page shows prompt cards. Click a card to see the full prompt text.

3. Copy the prompt

Press the Copy button on the card. The full prompt text is copied to your clipboard.

4. Paste it into your AI tool

Paste the prompt into your AI tool of choice — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, Leonardo AI, Ideogram, Veo, Runway, Seedance or Kling — and generate.

5. Check the preview image

Every prompt card has an AI-generated preview image showing what the prompt creates, so you can see the style before you use it.

6. Read the blog

The blog has short guides on writing better prompts — for portraits, cinematic videos and more.

Tips for better results

  • Change the subject, style or lighting words to make a prompt your own.
  • Keep the prompt's structure — it is written to work as a whole.
  • If a result is not right, change one detail at a time and try again.
