Funny AI Prompts

Funny prompts turn a simple idea into a joke picture or a short comedy scene. Mix an ordinary object with an odd situation and keep the details clear, so the humour lands. These prompts are good for memes, reels and posts you want people to share.

How to write better funny prompts

  • Keep one joke per prompt so the idea is easy to read.
  • Use a clear, simple setting so the funny part stands out.
  • Add an exaggerated expression to make the humour obvious.
