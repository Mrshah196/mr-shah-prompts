What you can do

Post to many Pages at once

Publish the same text post to all your selected Facebook Pages in a single click — no copy-paste, no switching Pages.

Attach a photo or video

Add a photo or video to your post with the upload box. The attachment goes out with the post on every selected Page.

Choose Pages per post

Turn Pages ON or OFF for each post. Select all at once, or pick only the Pages that fit this post.

Free, no install

Completely free and runs in your browser. There is no app to install and no account to create on this site.

Your data stays in your browser

Your login tokens and Page list are saved only in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to or stored on any server.

See the result for every Page

After publishing, the tool shows the result for each Page — published, or the error if something went wrong.

How it works

  1. Log in with Facebook — Open the tool and click "Login with Facebook". Your Pages load automatically; there is nothing to paste.
  2. Select Pages — Turn ON the Pages you want this post to go to. Turn OFF the ones you want to skip.
  3. Write & Publish — Type your post, attach a photo or video if you like, and click "Publish Now". Done.

Frequently asked questions

Is Multi-Page Post Publisher free?

Yes, it is completely free. There is no subscription, no trial and no limit on how many Pages you publish to.

Do I have to paste a token to use it?

No. You just log in with Facebook and your Pages load automatically. There is nothing to paste.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser's local storage. Your login tokens and Page data are never sent to or stored on any server. You can remove them at any time with Disconnect in the tool.

Can I post to Facebook Groups with it?

No, only Facebook Pages. Meta removed the Groups API and the publish_to_groups permission on 22 April 2024, so no tool can fetch Groups or publish to them through the official API. To post in a Group, publish from the tool to your Pages and share the post into the Group yourself.

Good to know. The tool is currently in development mode, so Facebook login works for app admins and testers for now. Public access for everyone opens after Meta App Review approval. Your login stays only in your browser either way.
