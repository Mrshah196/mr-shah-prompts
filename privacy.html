Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Mr Shah Prompts is a community library where people discover, copy and share AI prompts. This page explains what information the website handles.

Information we collect

  • Prompts you submit: the title, prompt text, category and the author name you type. Submissions are visible to other visitors after review.
  • Usage counts: likes and copy counts for prompts are stored so popular prompts can be shown.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are used. It may use cookies and collects anonymous usage data such as device type and approximate location.
  • On your device: your browser remembers which prompts you liked and your theme choice (local storage). This stays on your device.

How we use it

To run the website, show and organise prompts, prevent duplicates and spam, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Supabase, for storing prompts.
  • Google Analytics, for anonymous statistics.
  • Netlify, for hosting the website.

Your choices

Do not include personal or private details in prompts you submit, because they may be shown publicly. You can clear your browser storage at any time, and you can block analytics cookies in your browser settings. To request removal of a prompt you submitted, contact us through our WhatsApp channel.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last changed.
