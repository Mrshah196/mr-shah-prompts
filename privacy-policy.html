Privacy Policy — Mr Shah FB Publisher

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Mr Shah FB Publisher ("the App", Facebook App ID 1576761261159357) is operated by Mr Shah and published from mrshahai.com. This page explains what the App does, what information it uses, and how you can delete your data.

What the App does

The App lets you publish posts to the Facebook Pages you manage — text posts, photos and videos — from your own browser. You choose which of your Pages receive each post.

What data the App uses

  • Your Facebook Pages list: the names and IDs of the Pages you manage, fetched from Facebook when you log in with Facebook.
  • Page access tokens: when you log in with Facebook, Facebook gives the App short-lived tokens that let the App publish to your Pages on your behalf. They are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Post content you type: the text, photos or videos you choose to publish. They are sent directly from your browser to Facebook (graph.facebook.com) and are not stored by us.

Your tokens and Page data are never sent to our servers. The App has no server of its own and stores nothing about you anywhere — everything stays in your browser's local storage until you remove it.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your Facebook password.
  • We do not use or store the App Secret anywhere in the App.
  • We do not collect, store or sell any of your personal information on a server.
  • We do not share your data with any third party, except Facebook itself when you publish a post.

How to delete your data

  • In the App: open the tool and press Disconnect. This deletes your saved token, your Pages list and your publishing history from your browser.
  • On Facebook: go to Facebook Settings → Apps and websites → remove "Mr Shah Publisher". This immediately revokes the App's access to your Pages.
  • In your browser: you can also clear the site's local storage at any time from your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Facebook (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — the App uses the Facebook Graph API (graph.facebook.com) to list your Pages and publish your posts. Your use of Facebook is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics for the website (see the website Privacy Policy).

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
