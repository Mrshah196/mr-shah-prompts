AI YouTube Thumbnail Prompts

Thumbnail prompts are about stopping the scroll: one bold subject, huge emotion and almost no clutter. Describe the face, the gesture and two punchy background words, and keep space for title text. These prompts work with ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Midjourney and Flux, and export cleanly at 16:9. Copy any prompt, swap the subject and test two versions to see which gets the click.

How to write better youtube thumbnail prompts

  • Use one expressive face and one clear gesture.
  • Keep the background to two bold elements max.
  • Leave empty space on one side for title text.
